The process

How a swap actually happens

Three steps. No invoice, ever.

01

List what you know

Add a skill you can teach and one you're hoping to learn — takes about two minutes.

02

Get matched

SkillSwap surfaces people whose "wants" line up with your "offers," and vice versa.

03

Trade sessions

Message, agree on a time, and meet — in person or on a call. Rate each other after.
