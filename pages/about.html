Our mission

Learning without a price tag

Traditional education is expensive, and not everyone has the money to hire a private tutor or take a paid course. But everyone has skills — a language, a craft, a technical ability — that someone else would love to learn. SkillSwap exists to connect those people directly, cutting out the price tag entirely and replacing it with a fair trade of time and knowledge.

We believe the best learning happens person-to-person, built on trust, curiosity, and mutual benefit — not a transaction.
